Створити карту зацікавлених осіб для проекту “Піцерія”

The customer has a pizzeria chain in an English-speaking country. He wants to create a website for ordering pizza delivery online. The website must be
very simple but attractive to people. The customer wants to have:
•List of items with the picture, price, and short description. By clicking on an item, a new page with a bigger picture, a full description, the price,
ingredients, and other information are shown.
•Simple filters and sorting.
•Availability to make an order with payment to the courier (not on the website).
•Nice to have:
•Payment by credit card.
•Availability to use discount cards
